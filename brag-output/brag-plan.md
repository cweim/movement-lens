# Brag Plan: Movement Lens

## What is this app?
Movement Lens automatically clusters a football player's movement patterns from broadcast footage, lets an analyst review and label them, maps clusters across the full match timeline, then calls Microsoft AI to generate an evidence-backed interpretation grounded in real sports-science research — all exportable as a shareable evidence pack.

## The angle
This is real sports science powered by AI. Not a mockup. One match, one player (Marcelo, Real Madrid vs Real Sociedad), 290 observed windows of actual movement — clustered, reviewed, explained, and exported. The video shows the working tool doing the work: broadcast video in, movement evidence out.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cropped broadcast frame of Marcelo mid-stride with skeleton overlay, then the text: "290 windows. One player. Every movement pattern." — fast, visual, specific.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The cluster view: two cluster cards with video thumbnails playing, stats showing "60% of sample" / "40% of sample" — the AI found two movement groups.
- The match timeline: colored marks across 90 minutes — where does each cluster appear in the match?
- The "Explain with Microsoft AI" button press → the AI draft appearing: observed pattern, physical demands, recovery context — each section with real research citations.

## Outro / punchline
"Cluster evidence pack ready. Download ZIP." — the final output. Then the Movement Lens wordmark with tagline "Observed patterns. Reviewed categories. Research-backed context."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Clusters** — the app finds K=2 clusters, shows representative clips with skeleton overlays, stats per cluster.
2. **Match Timeline** — follow the pattern across the match. Colored marks on a 90-minute SVG timeline.
3. **Explain with Microsoft AI** — AI reads cluster stats, examples, timeline and research. Returns structured interpretation with citations. Export as evidence pack ZIP.
