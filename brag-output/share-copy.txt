Movement Lens — data-driven movement analysis from broadcast footage.

One match. One player. 290 observed windows of movement. Zero wearable sensors.

Movement Lens extracts pose data from broadcast video, clusters movement patterns, maps them across the full match, then calls Microsoft AI to explain what it sees — with cited sports-science research. Every insight is grounded in evidence, from pose estimation to AI interpretation to exportable evidence packs.

Built for coaches and players who want to understand movement — not just measure it.

Built for the Microsoft Premier League Hackathon.
